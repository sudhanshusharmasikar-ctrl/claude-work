Connecting to your account…

Small tasks like the ones in the online round: change a colour, move text, call the right function, show the date. For each task, read the short note, change the code, press Run to see it, then press Check. Do one stage a day; each takes about an hour.

The preview is a small browser inside this page. document, window, console.log and alert work as usual; alert messages also appear in the console. To practise on your laptop, press Copy as one file, paste it into a file called test.html and open it in Chrome.
