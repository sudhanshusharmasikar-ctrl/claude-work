<h1 class="front notoc">How to Use This Q&amp;A</h1>

These are the interview questions and answers from our study sessions, for the STRIKE GenAI lectures you have finished. Each answer is short enough to say in under a minute: interviewers want the idea, one example and the trade-off, not a lecture.

- **Part 1, Lectures 8–16:** embeddings, vector search (brute force, IVF, KD-tree, HNSW, PQ), RAG, advanced RAG and graph databases. **31 questions.**
- **Part 2, Lectures 20–21:** LangGraph: state, nodes, edges, reducers, checkpointers, resuming after a crash, and the three pipeline designs. **21 questions.**

:::tip How to practise
1. Cover the answer and say your own answer **out loud** in under 45 seconds.
2. Read the answer here and notice what you missed.
3. Do about **10 questions a day**, and mark the ones where you get stuck.
4. The next morning, read the notes pages for the marked ones, then try them again. The tag next to each question, like <span class="pref">Notes p.&nbsp;77</span>, is the page in **GenAI-RAG-Agents-Explained-Simply.pdf** that explains it in full.
:::

- **Part 2:** Q6–Q11 and Q13.
